About Us

Your Health, Our Priority

Advanced Medical Diagnostic Center is a leading diagnostic facility in Lahore, Pakistan, specializing in comprehensive medical screening and diagnostics. We serve international patients requiring medical checkups for visa applications, work permits, and general health assessments.

Our team of experienced medical professionals is dedicated to providing accurate diagnoses and quality healthcare services in a state-of-the-art facility.

Our Services

Medical Visa Screening

Comprehensive health checkups required for visa applications, work permits, and international travel documentation.

Laboratory Tests

Full range of blood tests, urinalysis, and pathological investigations using modern equipment.

Imaging Services

X-ray, ultrasound, and other imaging services for diagnostic purposes.

General Health Checkup

Comprehensive health assessment including physical examination and necessary investigations.

ECG & Cardiac Screening

Electrocardiogram and cardiac assessment for complete cardiovascular evaluation.

Report Generation

Fast and reliable report generation certified and ready for visa submission.
